# RL winning scenarios: post-hoc selected-scenario analysis

**This is a post-hoc selected-scenario analysis.** Scenarios are selected using their observed results; this does not establish that RL outperforms DCF/EDCA across all 40 scenarios or general Wi-Fi workloads.

## Exact selection criteria

A scenario is retained only if, in that same scenario, RL Adaptive DCF has strictly higher aggregate goodput (Flow 1 + Flow 2), strictly lower packet-loss percentage, and strictly lower average end-to-end latency than **both** DCF and EDCA. All six comparisons must pass. Differences no greater than the tolerance are treated as ties and do not qualify:

- Aggregate goodput: > 1e-05 Mbps advantage.
- Packet loss: > 1e-06 percentage-point advantage (lower for RL).
- Average latency: > 0.001 ms advantage (lower for RL).

Presentation scenario IDs (3): 1 (original scenario 5), 2 (original scenario 18), 3 (original scenario 24).

Retained: 3 scenarios. Excluded: 37 scenarios. Invalid or incomplete: 0. Valid but non-qualifying: 37.

Every retained scenario satisfies all three criteria against both baselines. The selected CSV includes all three methods for each retained scenario.

## Limitations

This is an outcome-conditioned, post-hoc subset of one recorded run per scenario, not an independent confirmatory evaluation. Selection can overstate apparent performance and does not justify generalization beyond the selected workloads. The original 40-scenario measurements, metrics, seeds, and checkpoints were not changed; no simulations were rerun and no model was retrained.
